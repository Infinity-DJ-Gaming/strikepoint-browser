Counter-Strike game content in assets/ is converted from the user's installed CS2.
Models, textures, animations, maps, sounds, fonts and Valve interface art remain
the property of Valve Corporation and their respective rights holders.
Conversion does not change ownership or grant an open-source asset license.

Asset extraction: Source 2 Viewer / ValveResourceFormat, https://s2v.app
https://github.com/ValveResourceFormat/ValveResourceFormat (MIT; notice in vendor/)

Browser libraries: Three.js, three-mesh-bvh and meshoptimizer. Their license
notices are retained in vendor/. Conversion scripts use glTF Transform and sharp.

This is an unofficial private browser practice adaptation, not a Valve release.

Update 10 experimental Apollo Royale includes original Fortnite terrain, map imagery, models and building recordings belonging to Epic Games. Community archive/Source-port credits, transformation details and hashes are retained in assets/royale/. POI compounds, material shading and some gameplay systems are reconstructed browser approximations. This is an unofficial crossover prototype, not an Epic release.
